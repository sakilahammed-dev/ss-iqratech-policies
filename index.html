1. Introduction

Welcome to SS IqraTech, an educational and Islamic science media initiative operated by Sakil Ahammed. We are committed to protecting the privacy, rights, and dignity of our viewers and community members.

This Privacy Policy explains how our automated Facebook Page application interacts with users who engage with our content, videos, and posts on Meta platforms.

2. Information We Process & Collect

We believe in minimal, ethical, and halal data handling (Sidq & Amanah). Our systems only interact with data that you publicly choose to share:

  • Public Comments & Feedback: When you leave a comment on our public posts or reels, our system reads the text to categorize viewer sentiment (such as praises, duas, questions, or appreciation).
  • Comment IDs: We temporarily store public comment IDs solely to ensure that our auto-response system does not send duplicate replies.
  • Public Name: If provided publicly by Meta Graph API, we use your name respectfully in our courtesy replies.

What We NEVER Collect

We do NOT collect, store, share, or sell any private personal information, passwords, financial records, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or private messages.

3. How We Use Information

The information processed is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To send warm, courteous, and Islamic adab replies (e.g., "জাযাকাল্লাহু খাইরান", "আল্লাহ আপনাকে কবুল করুন") to viewers who comment.
  • To like viewer comments from the official SS IqraTech page.
  • To automatically hide malicious links, spam, gambling, or offensive promotional comments to keep our community safe and wholesome.

4. User Data Deletion Instructions (Meta Compliance)

In accordance with Meta Platform Rules and global data protection standards (GDPR / CCPA), every user has the absolute right to have their data deleted:

  • Instant Deletion: You can delete your comment directly from Facebook at any time. Once deleted, our system loses all access to it immediately.
  • Manual Deletion Request: If you wish to request deletion of any activity records or references associated with your interaction with SS IqraTech, you can submit a request by:
    • Sending a direct message to our Facebook Page: SS IqraTech Official Page
    • Emailing our administrator at: sakilahammed.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request".
  • Upon receiving your request, all associated identifiers are permanently deleted from our cache within 24 to 48 hours.
